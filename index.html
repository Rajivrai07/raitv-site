Setup guide

Follow these simple steps — no problem, no confusion.

1

Download & install

Download the APK from above and install it. If your device asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser.

2

Open Addons

Open RAI TV, go to Settings → Addons. This is where the app gets its movies and shows from.

3

Add a source

Paste this addon URL in the box and tap Add / Install (Hindi movies, serials & shows):

https://manifest.desitvhub.eu.org/manifest.json

4

Enjoy!

That's it! Browse the library, pick a movie or show, and press play. 🍿
